**Geography break.** Editions 2023–2025 state that their data are based on the 2021 census geographic definitions, and the 2023 edition republished Oct-22 on that basis. Editions 2019–2022 carry no such note (earlier definitions, labelled `pre_2021_census`). 2022 is therefore kept twice in the history file, once per `geography_basis`; `is_primary_series` marks the 2021-census row for a continuous series. Within a basis the series are comparable; across the break they are not, most visibly for the universe.

**Revisions.** Every October that appears in two consecutive editions was compared. The only differences are Oct-22 (2022 edition vs 2023 edition), i.e. the geography revision. See the overlap table.

**Not used** (and why):

- Zone-level series: zones were redefined (`Zone 6 - Remainder of CMA` → `Zone 6 - Remainder of CMA` + `Zone 7 - Essex` in 2023 → `Zone 6 - North Essex County` + `Zone 7 - South Essex County` from 2024).
- Bachelor/studio detail: the label changed (`Bachelor` → `Studio`, 2025) and the CMA value is often suppressed.
- Tables 1.1.9, 3.1.7, 3.1.8, 3.1.9: absent from the 2019 edition, so 2019–2025 coverage is impossible.
- Table 1.1.5 (fixed-sample % change of rent): a change measure, not a level, and outside the priority list; left for a later decision.
- Oct-18 (previous column of the 2019 edition): outside the requested 2019–2025 window.
